Workout Plans & Splits

Choose a routine that matches your schedule and goal. These are example templates you can adapt to your own weight, sets, and reps.

Show plans for goal:

3-day full body (Beginner)

Good for busy students or beginners.

  • Day 1: Squats, Push-ups, Rows, Planks
  • Day 2: Lunges, Bench (or push-ups), Lat pulldown, Crunches
  • Day 3: Deadlifts (light), Shoulder press, Biceps curls, Triceps dips

4-day Upper / Lower

Great for fat loss with enough training volume.

  • Day 1: Upper (bench, rows, shoulder press, pull-ups)
  • Day 2: Lower (squats, RDL, leg press, calves)
  • Day 3: Upper (incline bench, pulldown, lateral raise, arms)
  • Day 4: Lower (front squats, lunges, hamstring curls, abs)

5-day Push / Pull / Legs

Popular for gaining size and strength.

  • Day 1: Push — chest, shoulders, triceps
  • Day 2: Pull — back, biceps
  • Day 3: Legs — quads, hamstrings, calves
  • Day 4: Push (variation)
  • Day 5: Pull/legs mix + core

Home-only fat-loss circuit

No equipment required.

  • Push-ups / incline push-ups
  • Bodyweight squats and lunges
  • Mountain climbers & jumping jacks
  • Planks and side planks

Always warm up and train safely. Ask a coach if you’re unsure about technique.
