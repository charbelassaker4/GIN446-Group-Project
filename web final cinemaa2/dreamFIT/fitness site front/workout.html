Training Programs

Structured routines for Cut, Maintain, and Bulk. Always warm up properly, use a weight you can control with good form, and stop if you feel pain.

Cut Program (5 days/week)

Higher reps, short rest, supports a calorie deficit.

  • Day 1 – Upper A: Bench 4×12, Rows 4×15, Shoulder press 3×12, Curls 3×15, Triceps extensions 3×15
  • Day 2 – Lower A: Squats 4×15, RDL 4×12, Lunges 3×15, Leg press 3×20, Calf raises 4×20
  • Day 3 – HIIT/Cardio: Burpees, mountain climbers, jumping jacks (20–30 min)
  • Day 4 – Upper B: Incline bench 4×12, Pulldown 4×15, Lateral raises 4×15, Hammer curls 3×12, Dips 3×12
  • Day 5 – Lower B: Leg press 4×15, Front squat 3×12, Hamstring curls 4×15, Abs circuit 3–4 rounds

Maintain Program (3–4 days/week)

Balanced training with moderate intensity.

  • Day 1 – Full Body A: Squat 3×8, Bench 3×10, Row 3×10, Overhead press 3×8
  • Day 2 – Full Body B: Deadlift 3×5, Pulldown 3×12, Dip 3×10, Leg press 3×12
  • Day 3 – Full Body C: Incline press 3×10, RDL 3×8, Rear delts 3×15, Curls 3×12
  • Optional Day 4 – Conditioning: Sled pushes, planks, farmer carries, light cardio

Bulk Program (Push / Pull / Legs / Upper / Lower)

Higher volume with progressive overload.

  • Day 1 – Push: Bench 5×5, OHP 4×6, Dips 3×10, Lateral raises 4×15, Triceps pushdown 3×12
  • Day 2 – Pull: Deadlift 5×5, Barbell rows 4×8, Face pulls 3×15, Curls 3×12, Rear delts 3×15
  • Day 3 – Legs: Squat 5×5, Leg press 4×10, RDL 4×8, Calf raises 4×15
  • Day 4 – Upper Hypertrophy: Incline bench 4×10, Pulldown 4×12, Chest fly 3×12, Side raises 3×15
  • Day 5 – Lower Hypertrophy: Hack squat 4×10, Lunges 4×12, Hamstring curl 4×12, Abs circuit
